Store policies

Last updated October 7, 2026. Questions? Email roman.proposal@gmail.com.

Shipping

  • Free shipping on every order to US addresses (including APO/FPO where the carrier allows). We don't ship outside the US right now.
  • Items ship from partner warehouses in the United States. Orders are usually packed within 1–3 business days. Delivery usually takes 3–8 business days in total.
  • If you order several items, they may arrive in separate packages.
  • Every order is trackable. Use your order number on the tracking page. Tracking appears once the package ships.
  • If your order hasn't arrived within 20 business days of purchase, email us and we'll refund you in full.

Returns & refunds

  • Damaged, defective, or wrong item: email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a full refund to your original payment method. You don't need to ship anything back.
  • Lost package: if tracking shows no delivery within 20 business days, we refund in full.
  • Change of mind: to cancel, email us within 12 hours of ordering and we'll stop the order if it hasn't been packed yet, then refund you in full. Because items ship directly from our warehouse partners, we can't accept change-of-mind returns once an order has shipped.
  • If an item sells out after you pay, we refund you automatically and email you a receipt.
  • Approved refunds are issued within 3 business days. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.

Prices & discounts

  • All prices are in US dollars and include shipping. Sales tax, if any, is calculated at checkout.
  • Bundle discount: when your bag has 2 or more items, every item is discounted by the percentage shown in the store (currently 15%). The discount is applied automatically at checkout.
  • Prices follow our suppliers' costs and may change at any time. The price shown at checkout is the price you pay.

Privacy

  • We collect what's needed to deliver your order: name, shipping address, email, and phone number (carriers use it for delivery issues).
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Your shipping details are shared with our fulfilment partner and the delivery carrier only to ship your order. We don't sell your information.
  • If the store uses the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel, it records page views and purchases so we can measure our ads. You can control ad tracking in your Facebook/Instagram ad settings and browser.
  • Your bag is saved in your own browser so it's there when you come back. Clear your browser storage to remove it.
  • To ask what we hold about you or have it deleted, email us.

Terms of sale

  • By placing an order you confirm the shipping address is correct. We aren't responsible for packages sent to an address entered incorrectly at checkout, but we'll help where the carrier allows.
  • Product photos and descriptions come from our suppliers. Colours and sizes may vary slightly. If an item is materially different from its description, our returns policy above applies.
  • We may cancel and fully refund any order, for example if an item becomes unavailable or a pricing error occurs.
  • Items are for personal use. Follow any safety instructions included with the product.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of the United States and the state in which the seller is based.
