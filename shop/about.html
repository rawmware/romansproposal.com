About Northline Finds

A small curated shop for trending products that are actually worth buying.

Northline Finds started with a simple frustration: trending-product stores that sell junk at inflated prices, hide their shipping times, and disappear the moment something goes wrong. We're doing the opposite.

How we choose products

  • Useful first. If we wouldn't buy it ourselves or recommend it to a friend, we don't list it.
  • Vetted suppliers. Every product comes from a supplier we've checked for reliability, accurate listings, and consistent quality.
  • Honest pricing. No fake "was $199, now $19.99" markdowns. The price you see is a fair price.

How ordering works

When you place an order, it's fulfilled directly by our supply partners — that's how we keep prices low without a warehouse markup. Your order ships within 1–2 business days and arrives in 7–12 business days, with tracking emailed to you the moment it leaves the warehouse.

Our promise

  • 30-day returns, no questions asked
  • Real human support at roman.proposal@gmail.com
  • Secure payments processed by Stripe — we never see your card details
