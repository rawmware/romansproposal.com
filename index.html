FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers.

Do I own everything you build?

Yes — 100%. The code, the domain, and every account are set up in your name. If we ever part ways, everything keeps running and you can hand it to anyone.

What does “bring your own key” mean for the AI parts?

You create your own AI provider account and API key; I wire everything to it. You pay the provider directly — usually a few dollars a month for a small business — with zero markup from me and no lock-in. Swap providers or keys anytime.

How fast is “days, not months” really?

Most projects go live in 1–2 weeks. A starter site can ship in about a week. You’ll see a working first version within days of kickoff, not a mockup in a slide deck.

How do we start?

Run the proposal builder above, hit send, and I’ll reply within one business day with a fixed quote. One short call to align, then I build.

I already have an application. Can you work with it?

Usually, yes. I can rebuild it properly or add just the pieces you’re missing — an AI assistant, lead automation, or a booking flow — without touching what already works.

What does it cost to keep running?

Very little. Hosting for these applications is typically low-cost, and AI usage is billed by your provider at cost — most small businesses land in the single digits per month. If you become a client, I’ll walk through the exact numbers with you.
