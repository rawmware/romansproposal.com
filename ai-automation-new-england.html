Questions before we start

Do you work with an existing application?

Often, yes. We first look at what it supports, who owns it, and what needs to connect. The proposal can cover an integration or a single workflow.

What does it cost?

Project pricing is agreed after scope is clear. The homepage lists the current one-on-one training options. Provider usage, hosting, and other ongoing costs are discussed separately so you know what you will own and pay for.

Roman also runs AI Actually Help’s free community workshops for nonprofits and the people they serve. That program has its own learning resources and workshop request process.
